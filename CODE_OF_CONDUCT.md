# Community code of conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and patient with people testing or contributing to Frameway. Critique ideas and code rather than people. Welcome different backgrounds and experience levels, and assume good intent while taking reported harm seriously.

Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, sexual harassment, doxxing, and publishing private information without permission are not acceptable. Do not post credentials, customer data, or sensitive recordings in project discussions.

The project maintainer, [NicholasMicali](https://github.com/NicholasMicali), is responsible for enforcement in project issues, PRs, and discussions. Depending on severity and repetition, responses may include asking for a change, removing content, closing a discussion, or restricting participation. Decisions should be proportionate and explain the behavior at issue.

For a concern that can be discussed publicly, open an issue addressed to the maintainer. To request confidential handling, open an issue saying only that you need a private conduct-reporting channel; do not include names, allegations, or sensitive details. The maintainer will arrange a private channel before collecting the report. Do not use security advisories for ordinary conduct reports.
