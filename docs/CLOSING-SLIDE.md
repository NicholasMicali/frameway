# Closing slides

Choose **Add closing slide** below the editor's step list to append an optional completion screen. Edit its headline and description in the settings panel. It uses the project's colors, typography, and logo in a centered layout, with a **Replay walkthrough** button.

Existing projects are unchanged until you add one. The last step's ordinary ending is connected to the new slide; explicit branches and earlier endings are preserved. If your walkthrough branches, use **Continue to** on the relevant step to route it to the closing slide.

A closing slide ends its playback route even if moved into the middle of the list. Replay starts at the first step, including a title slide when present. Normal step navigation remains available under the existing video playback rules. Closing slides support duplication, deletion, and undo/redo.

Closing slides work in preview, standalone HTML, embed folders, and editable project backups. Backups containing a closing slide require this version or newer; older backups still import without a closing slide being added automatically.
